spdhost for Android — notices
=============================

This app is a front-end to the spdhost core from
https://github.com/Seuj09/Spd_dump_termux (no-root/). The app and the core
are released under the MIT License below.

The core binary shipped inside this APK (lib/<abi>/libspdhost.so) is a
static executable built with zig cc against musl libc and libusb:

  * libusb 1.0 (v1.0.27, https://github.com/libusb/libusb)
    GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later.
    libusb is statically linked into libspdhost.so. The complete source of
    spdhost, the exact libusb tag and the build scripts that link them
    (no-root/Makefile, no-root/scripts/cross-arm32.sh) are published at
    https://github.com/Seuj09/Spd_dump_termux, so you can rebuild and relink
    the core with a modified libusb and drop it into the APK's jniLibs.
    The LGPL-2.1 text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.txt

  * musl libc (https://musl.libc.org) — MIT License.

The app itself uses AndroidX (core, activity, lifecycle) and Jetpack Compose
(ui, material3), Apache License 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

FDL loaders, stub binaries and firmware images are NOT shipped in this APK.
